Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Maarifat-ul-Uloom Academy ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information you provide when you visit our website or contact us to enquire about our courses.

Information We Collect

When you use our Contact form, join our email waitlist, or message us via WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if provided)

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, schedule a free trial class, communicate about enrollment, and — if you opt in — notify you about new course launches. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Communication

We may contact you by email, phone, or WhatsApp using the details you provide, in order to follow up on your enquiry or trial class request.

Data Storage

Information submitted through our forms is stored securely and retained only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry or maintain your enrollment records.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at maarifatululoom@gmail.com.

Cookies

Our website may use minimal, non-invasive local storage to remember your light/dark theme preference. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Third-Party Links

Our website links to third-party platforms (including Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, X, and WhatsApp). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external platforms.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at maarifatululoom@gmail.com.
